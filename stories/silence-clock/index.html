The Situation

What It Usually Means

His silence is a pattern, not a mystery. How he left tells you more than how long it has been.

The Silence Clock

Ask three things. How did he leave? Has he come back before -- and actually changed? Does his contact carry substance, or only loneliness?

What To Watch For

A return with no change is usually just a repeat. Let his effort carry more weight than his timing.

The Complete Short Answer

Sometimes he comes back, and sometimes he does not -- no honest reader promises either. What can be read is the pattern, and what would be different this time.
